# 002 — Blind stratified owner-verdict batch to clear the 15+15 separation gate before any bar or prompt change

## Council reasoning
Every downstream question the lab wants to answer is blocked by the same missing input: owner labels. Gen 1 measured separation on 2 positive and 2 negative verdicts (AUC 0.5, gap 0.0485), which is below the 15+15 gate and therefore no evidence either way, and Gen 2 added no labels at all (separation: null) while showing that the notify decision is where the action is — notify_rate 0.197 on 122 items, band_count 18 (band_density 0.148), 14 notify_flips, and three interests producing almost nothing (personal-knowledge-learning 0/11 at bar 0.80, emdr-trauma-therapy 0/13 at bar 0.78, behavioral-psychology 1/18 at bar 0.80) against nbis-nebius 10/22 at bar 0.70. Without labels we cannot tell whether those near-zero interests are correct strictness or miscalibrated bars, cannot judge whether the 14 flips (mean_drift 0.0569, median 0.0345) harm or help the owner, and cannot justify either bar fitting or further prompt tuning. Jitter is not the bottleneck (mean_std 0.0106, max_std 0.0221) and drift attribution is still unavailable anyway (strict_within_version_n 0), so the smallest high-value change is a single measurement generation: a blind, stratified owner-verdict batch that clears the 15+15 gate and produces a bootstrapped AUC on data no prompt was tuned against. That number decides the next generation: AUC clears the bar and the lab moves to bar fitting; it does not, and the lab must fix the scorer's dimensions before touching bars.

## Evidence (measured, from lab state)
- Separation has never been tested: gen 1 had n_positive 2, n_negative 2, AUC 0.5 (0.25 resolution) and a 0.0485 gap that is only ~2.3x jitter; gen 2 reported separation null. Meanwhile gen 2 shows the decision surface is real and uneven: corpus_n 122, notify_rate 0.197, band_count 18, band_density 0.148, notify_flips 14, mean_drift 0.0569 (median 0.0345), with per-interest yields ranging from nbis-nebius 10/22 (bar 0.70) and narcolepsy-eds 7/23 (bar 0.74) down to behavioral-psychology 1/18 (bar 0.80), emdr-trauma-therapy 0/13 (bar 0.78) and personal-knowledge-learning 0/11 (bar 0.80) - 42 items yielding 1 notification. Nothing measured so far can distinguish correct strictness from bar miscalibration in those three interests, or say whether the 14 flips move items toward or away from what the owner wants. Jitter is not the constraint (mean_std 0.0106, max_std 0.0221, flip_rate 0.0, the last uninformative because gen-1 items sat 0.20-0.21 below their bars). Gen 2 also partly relieves the gen-1 mode-collapse worry: dim_distinct_values are 22-34 across dimensions, so no dimension is constant, though personal_relevance is the coarsest at 22 distinct values and had the second-lowest gen-1 jitter (0.0085). Drift attribution remains impossible this generation (strict_within_version_n 0), which is a further reason not to spend the next generation there.

## Changes (smallest set that tests the idea)
- Change exactly one thing: add a label-capture stage and run this generation as measurement only - no prompt edit, no bar edit, no re-scoring. (a) Build the label set from the existing gen-2 rescore of all 122 items: all 18 band items; all remaining above-bar items (~24 notify total, minus band overlap); and ~30 below-bar items sampled with deliberate overweight on behavioral-psychology, personal-knowledge-learning and emdr-trauma-therapy (their 42 items are where the 0.0/0.056 notify rates live), plus below-bar items from the other five interests. Target ~65-70 unique items. (b) Present each item to the owner BLIND: title/content only, no score, no dimension values, no interest bar, no notify status, order randomized across interests. Collect one binary verdict per item ('would want this surfaced' / 'would not'), optional free-text reason, no numeric rating. (c) Re-show ~10% of items (7-8) later in the sequence as duplicates to get a code-computed intra-rater agreement figure. (d) Stamp the label batch itself with model claude-opus-5 and prompt_hash 92954b87de02 so the labels stay attributable to the scores they are being evaluated against; do not re-open corpus-wide stamping or drift decomposition this generation (proposal 1 territory). (e) Freeze the batch as a test split: it may be used to evaluate, never to fit bars or edit prompts in the same generation that produced it.

## Pre-registered prediction
Pre-registered, on labels that did not exist when any current prompt or bar was set: with at least 15 positive and 15 negative verdicts collected, the code-computed AUC of the current stored score against the owner verdict will be >= 0.70, with a bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0.55 - a move up from the uninformative gen-1 AUC 0.5 that will now be resolvable rather than quantized at 0.25. Secondary, decision-relevant prediction: among the 42 items in behavioral-psychology, personal-knowledge-learning and emdr-trauma-therapy, the owner-positive rate will be >= 0.15 (>= 6 items) versus the 1 item those interests currently notify, indicating bar level rather than item supply suppresses their notify rates of 0.056/0.0/0.0. Report intra-rater agreement on the duplicate pairs as a code-computed number alongside both.

## Validation plan (data the change was not designed against)
Compute, all in code: (1) AUC with 2000-sample bootstrap 95% CI, reported only if n_positive >= 15 AND n_negative >= 15; if either falls short, extend the batch rather than report - no verdict-separation claim below the gate. (2) The same AUC restricted to the 18 band items, reported ONLY if that subset independently clears 15+15; otherwise report band positive-rate as a descriptive count with no separation claim. (3) Owner-positive rate per interest with exact counts, against each interest's current bar. (4) For the 14 notify_flips, the owner verdict on each flipped item, so the sign of drift's effect is measured rather than assumed. (5) Intra-rater agreement on duplicates. Set delta_vs_baseline to null deliberately and record that this generation is measurement, not an A/B. Decision rule fixed in advance: AUC CI lower bound > 0.55 routes the next generation to per-interest bar fitting on the calibration half of the labels with the held-out half reserved; CI including 0.5 routes it to scorer/dimension redesign starting with personal_relevance (22 distinct values, jitter 0.0085) and blocks bar fitting entirely.

## Overfitting risks
Range restriction: labeling only band items would compress score variance and depress AUC artificially - hence the far-from-bar strata, and hence AUC is computed on the full labeled set first. Anchoring: showing scores, bars or notify status would make labels a readback of the scorer instead of an independent signal; blinding is the whole point and any unblinded label must be discarded. Selection bias: labeling only notified items would undersample negatives and inflate apparent performance; negatives come from the below-bar strata by construction. Rater drift and order effects: one long sitting can shift the owner's threshold mid-batch, which the duplicate re-shows are there to detect. Fitting-on-test: the strongest risk is using this batch to both fit bars and report AUC in the same generation - the batch is frozen as evaluation-only, and bar fitting next generation must use a split with a reserved held-out portion. Finally, an AUC computed on ~30-40 labels has wide CIs; the bootstrap interval, not the point estimate, is the reportable quantity.

## Rollback trigger
Revert this design change (and treat its output as unusable) if intra-rater agreement on the duplicate pairs is < 0.80, or if fewer than 15 positive or 15 negative verdicts are obtained after extending the batch once - in either case no AUC is reported and the label protocol itself is redesigned. Revert the lab's working assumption that the scorer tracks owner taste, and cancel all planned bar-fitting and prompt-tuning work, if the bootstrap 95% CI for AUC includes 0.5 or its lower bound is <= 0.55; the next generation then targets the dimensions themselves rather than the bars. If the secondary prediction fails (owner-positive rate < 0.15 in the three near-zero interests), leave bars 0.80/0.80/0.78 untouched - their low yield is then measured as correct, not miscalibrated.
