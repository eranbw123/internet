# 001 — Pin and stamp the scorer, then re-score the stored corpus to decompose drift and read out band density

## Council reasoning
The only large, real, code-computed effect in generation 1 is drift: mean_drift 0.0535 is ~5x mean_std 0.0106 and comparable to the width between interest bars (0.70-0.85), while jitter (mean_std 0.0106, max_std 0.0221) sits well under tolerance and is therefore not the bottleneck. Every other reading is gated out: separation cannot be claimed at 2 positive / 2 negative labels (AUC 0.5 has 0.25 resolution and contradicts the 0.0485 gap, itself only ~2.3x jitter), and flip_rate 0.0 is an artifact of sampling items 0.21 and 0.20 below their bars, so no band claim is licensed either. With delta_vs_baseline null, no prompt change is attributable to anything yet, so a prompt variant now would confound the one measurable defect. The single smallest high-value change is therefore instrumentation, not tuning: pin and stamp the scorer (prompt hash, model version, decoding params, retrieval snapshot), then run one re-score pass over the full stored corpus to split mean_drift into a within-version and a cross-version component, with per-interest score-vs-bar distributions as a free co-product. That one pass both tests a falsifiable claim about where the 0.0535 comes from and tells the lab whether E3 should be band-proximate stress testing or bar fitting.

## Evidence (measured, from lab state)
- Gen 1 (code-computed): mean_drift 0.0535 is ~5x mean_std 0.0106 and comparable to the spacing of interest bars (0.70-0.85) ג€” the largest real effect in the run. Jitter is not the bottleneck: mean_std 0.0106 and max_std 0.0221 are 2-5x under the 0.05 tolerance, with variance concentrated in surprise (0.0253) and specificity (0.0152) while personal_relevance (0.0085), importance (0.0075) and depth (0.0125) are near-deterministic, and several dim_std values of exactly 0.0 are unexplained. flip_rate 0.0 carries no band information: sampled items sit 0.21 and 0.20 below their bars and only item 91 is within ~2.5 std of its bar. Separation is untested, not refuted: 2 positive and 2 negative labels, AUC 0.5 at 0.25 resolution, gap 0.0485 (~2.3x jitter). delta_vs_baseline is null, so no prompt change is currently attributable to any measured effect. Budget used: 31.

## Changes (smallest set that tests the idea)
- One instrumentation change, no prompt edits. (1) Stamp every stored and newly written score with prompt_hash, model_version, decoding params (temperature, top_p), and retrieval/context snapshot id; backfill pre-existing stored scores as stamp=unstamped rather than guessing. (2) Freeze the current prompt for this generation (no rubric edits, no surprise anchors, no justification-before-number variant). (3) Run exactly one re-score pass over the full stored corpus: 1 sample per item, fresh session per item, production temperature, independent seed, response cache disabled and asserted off. (4) Emit code-computed metrics only: within_version_drift = mean |rescore - stored| over items whose stamp matches the current prompt_hash+model_version; cross_version_drift = same over known-mismatched stamps; unstamped_drift reported separately; per-interest score distribution against that interest's bar, yielding notify_rate (fraction >= bar) and band_density (fraction within +/-0.05 of bar) and the absolute count of band-proximate items per interest; distinct-value count per dimension across the corpus to test whether the 0.0 dim_std dimensions are constant or discriminating.

## Pre-registered prediction
Pre-registered, measured on the full stored corpus rather than the 10 sampled items, and on stored scores written before the stamp existed: within_version_drift mean <= 0.020 (i.e. within ~2x the carried-over mean_std 0.0106), and the cross-version plus unstamped strata account for the bulk of the current figure, with cross_version_drift >= 0.045 against mean_drift 0.0535. Secondary, non-reverting routing readout: given mean_positive 0.767 and mean_negative 0.7185 against bars of 0.70-0.85, expect corpus notify_rate < 0.25 and band_density < 0.20.

## Validation plan (data the change was not designed against)
All metrics code-computed and reported per interest as well as in aggregate. Gates enforced: report no AUC, gap, or separation claim until >= 15 positive and >= 15 negative owner verdicts exist (currently 2 and 2) ג€” keep collecting labels passively, make no claim; report no flip_rate or threshold-band claim unless >= 15 items within +/-0.05 of their bar are actually found; do not recompute jitter ג€” carry gen-1 mean_std 0.0106 forward with its ~40% sampling error (repeats = 3) as the reference scale for drift. Routing rule fixed in advance: if band_density >= 0.20, E3 is the stratified band-proximate repeat design (>= 15 near-bar items, ~10 far controls, 5 repeats each, fresh session per repeat); if band_density < 0.20, E3 is bar fitting/calibration and prompt tuning is deferred. The frozen surprise-rubric A/B is queued for E4 at the earliest, and only as a populated A/B with delta_vs_baseline non-null against a stamped baseline.

## Overfitting risks
A re-score under the identical prompt can look artificially low-drift through caching, shared sessions, or reduced-temperature decoding ג€” mitigated by fresh session, independent seed, production temperature, and an explicit cache-off assertion; if these cannot be verified, the within_version_drift number is void rather than favorable. Stamping is retrospective, so unstamped items may silently include same-version scores and inflate cross_version_drift ג€” hence unstamped is reported as its own stratum and never merged into cross_version_drift. Single pass per item entangles drift with jitter; the entanglement is bounded by the carried-over 0.0106, not re-estimated, and no jitter claim is made. band_density and notify_rate are measured against bars that are themselves the suspected defect, so they are descriptive routing inputs, not evidence about scorer quality. No prompt variant is run this generation precisely because changing the prompt while attributing version-driven drift would confound delta_vs_baseline with the quantity being decomposed.

## Rollback trigger
Revert the stamping/pinning instrumentation and its interpretation if within_version_drift mean > 0.040 (less than a 25% reduction against mean_drift 0.0535), or if cross_version_drift < within_version_drift ג€” either result means drift is not prompt/model-version attributable and the next probe must target input-side non-determinism (retrieval and context assembly) instead. Also revert if the re-score pass cannot cover >= 80% of the stored corpus within the remaining budget, since a partial pass supports neither the drift decomposition nor the corpus-wide notify_rate readout.
