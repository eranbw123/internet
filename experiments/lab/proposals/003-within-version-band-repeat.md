## Council reasoning
The council settles on this: gen-2's rescore already answered the distribution question label-free (notify_rate 0.197, band_count 18, band_density 0.148) and refuted the mode-collapse worry (dim_distinct_values 22-34 across 122 items), but it left the one decision-relevant quantity unresolved — strict_within_version_n is 0, so the 0.0569 mean drift and the 14/122 notify_flips cannot be split between scorer nondeterminism and genuine cross-version change. Gen-1's jitter figure (mean_std 0.0106, flip_rate 0.0) does not settle it, because those 10 items were far from their bars and the run used only 3 possibly-shared repeats; the 18 band items now identified are the first sample that clears the >=15 band-proximate gate. The Skeptic's confound is decisive: if a second pinned draw were run in a shared session or with prompt caching, low variance would be an artifact, so the control stratum doubles as a leak detector. The single change is therefore a pinned, fresh-session, 3-repeat within-version measurement on 18 band items plus 22 far controls (120 calls), traded against deleting the standalone far-from-bar jitter experiment, the label-gated separation/AUC block the owner has indefinitely deferred, and the unattributable stored-vs-current drift metric. Bar calibration is the obviously higher-value question, but it is label-gated in substance and premature until a score is shown to be reproducible enough for a bar to mean anything.

## Pre-registered prediction
Pre-registered before the run, measured on fresh independent draws that no part of this change was fitted to; the control stratum in particular was not used to set any parameter. Primary (code-computed): mean_abs_pairwise_delta over the 40-item set <= 0.025 and band-stratum mean_std <= 0.020 — i.e. within-version repeat variation accounts for under half of the 0.0569 cross-condition drift, implying most of the 14 notify_flips is cross-version change rather than nondeterminism. Secondary: control-stratum mean_std <= 0.015 (direction: up from gen-1's 0.0106 because sessions are now independent, but still well under the 0.05 tolerance) and control-stratum flip_rate <= 0.05. Third: band-stratum flip_rate strictly inside (0.10, 0.45) — non-zero, contradicting gen-1's flip_rate 0.0, which the band gate now makes measurable — while band-stratum mean_std stays within 1.5x control-stratum mean_std, i.e. instability at the bar comes from proximity, not from higher variance on band items. All three must hold for the change to stand.

## Validation plan (data the change was not designed against)
Gates checked before any claim: 18 band items >= the 15-item threshold, so band flip_rate claims are admissible; 3 repeats >= the repeat-3 threshold, so jitter claims are admissible; controls are >= 0.15 from their bars, so no band or threshold claim is drawn from them. No AUC, gap, or verdict-separation figure is computed or reported — that metric family is deleted for this generation. Compute per-stratum mean_std, max_std, mean_abs_pairwise_delta and flip_rate with a 1000-resample bootstrap CI over items, and report the per-dimension std split so the gen-1 finding that surprise (0.0253) and specificity (0.0152) carry most variance can be re-checked on band-proximate items. Success = all three predicted intervals hold; the lab then treats within-version reproducibility as established and the next open question as bar calibration, which stays parked while labels are deferred. Failure on any interval reverts this change per the rollback trigger. Do not change any prompt text, bar, or rubric this generation: delta_vs_baseline stays null by design, because this run measures reproducibility of a fixed configuration, not the effect of a variant.

## Overfitting risks
(1) Band membership is defined by a single gen-2 draw, so regression to the mean will inflate apparent band instability regardless of scorer quality; for that reason pass/fail rests on mean_std and mean_abs_pairwise_delta, and band flip_rate is bounded from both sides rather than minimized. (2) Prompt caching, a shared session, or a reused seed would deflate std toward zero and manufacture a pass — if control-stratum mean_std < 0.003 the run is declared invalid and re-executed rather than scored, since gen-1's 0.0106 may itself be an underestimate for exactly this reason. (3) 40 stratified items is not the corpus; the <= 0.025 figure must be reported as a stratified estimate with its bootstrap CI, never as a corpus measurement, and it does not license re-deriving a corpus-wide drift number. (4) Tuning any interest bar to move notify_rate 0.197, or to lift the two interests currently at 0.0 (personal-knowledge-learning n=11, emdr-trauma-therapy n=13), would be fitting to an owner preference that has not been expressed — explicitly out of scope this generation. (5) Reading a low band flip_rate as evidence the scorer tracks taste is a category error: this run measures reproducibility only, and separation remains untested in either direction.

## Rollback trigger
Revert — restore the deleted far-from-bar baseline experiment, unpin, and make scorer determinism rather than calibration the next question — if any of: band-stratum mean_std >= 0.045 (approaching the 0.05 tolerance, meaning band decisions are not reproducible under a fixed configuration); band-stratum flip_rate >= 0.50 (notifications near bars are effectively coin flips); or mean_abs_pairwise_delta >= 0.045, i.e. >= ~80% of the 0.0569 drift is reproduced within a single pinned version, which kills the cross-version explanation outright. Invalidate and re-run rather than score if control-stratum mean_std < 0.003 (cache or session leak). Treat the stratification model as wrong, and re-derive the band definition before any further band claim, if control-stratum flip_rate > 0.05 or if band-stratum mean_std exceeds 1.5x control-stratum mean_std.
