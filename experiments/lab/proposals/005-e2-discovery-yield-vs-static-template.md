## Pre-registered prediction
Measured on items that do not exist yet at proposal time -- a fresh collection window, no item from the current 122-item corpus, and the strategist never shown the rubric or bars -- Arm A beats Arm S on the three starved interests on both of: (1) above-bar count rises from the current 0-1/42 baseline to >= 4 above-bar items across the three starved interests in Arm A, with Arm A above-bar count strictly greater than Arm S's; (2) pooled p90 final_score across the three starved interests is >= 0.04 higher in Arm A than in Arm S. On nbis-nebius (positive control) the prediction is deliberately null-to-flat: no requirement that Arm A raise above-bar rate above the ~0.50 already observed, and a rise there is treated as uninformative about starvation. Directionally, if collection is the binding constraint the starved-interest score distributions shift up as a whole (p75 and p90 both move) rather than producing isolated above-bar outliers; if scoring is the binding constraint the two arms' distributions are indistinguishable no matter what the angles retrieve.

## Validation plan (data the change was not designed against)
Pre-registered before any item is collected. PRIMARY (decisive): Arm A above-bar count across the three starved interests >= 4 AND > Arm S's count. SECONDARY (decisive, must also hold): pooled p90 gap (A minus S) on the three starved interests >= 0.04. Both hold -> VALIDATED, the static template is retired and strategist angles become the production collector for all interests. Neither holds AND the pooled p90 gap is < 0.02 -> the retrieval hypothesis is falsified for these interests, Arm A is reverted to the static template, and the lab records that the zero-notify pathology is a scorer/bar property, which is label-gated -- the correct next state is idle until labels, not another label-free generation. GATES AND NON-CLAIMS: no verdict-separation or precision claim is made anywhere in this generation (no owner labels are collected, the 15+15 gate is untouched and remains unmet). Band/threshold-band claims are declared non-decisive: the within-0.05-of-bar counts are reported as diagnostics only and cannot trigger any outcome, since the starved interests currently hold 1 band item each. ## Overfitting risks
The dominant risk is Goodhart: the same scorer that judges the items is downstream of a strategist that could learn to fish for whatever the scorer rewards. This is why the strategist is denied the dimension names, the rubric, the bar values, and all past scores, and is given only the interest description -- if that firewall is breached the run is void, not positive. Second risk: an above-bar item is not a good item; label-free above-bar rate is a proxy for owner value and is only trustworthy while the scorer is trusted, so every above-bar item Arm A produces in a starved interest is parked in a labeled queue for the eventual bar-calibration generation rather than treated as a validated win. Third risk: a null result is genuinely ambiguous between 'bad angles' and 'domain-miscalibrated scorer' -- the pooled p90 shift is the disambiguator, and the < 0.02 gap clause is what converts an ambiguous null into a clean falsification and a decision to go idle rather than into another speculative retrieval generation. Fourth risk: nbis-nebius improving is the cheapest way for this proposal to look successful while answering nothing, which is why it is excluded from both decision criteria. Fifth risk, accepted deliberately: deleting the drift apparatus means a scorer regression would now be caught only by the non-decisive 2-item probe; this is judged acceptable because reproducibility was measured three ways and closed, and because re-adding a sentinel later is cheap.

## Rollback trigger
Revert Arm A and restore the static template as sole collector if, on the three starved interests, Arm A's above-bar count is <= Arm S's OR Arm A produces < 4 above-bar items, and the pooled p90 gap is < 0.02. Additionally revert immediately, regardless of yield, if cross-arm dedupe overlap >= 0.50 (the arms are not actually distinct), if the strategist output is found to reference dimension names, bar values, or past scores (firewall breach), or if the 40/113 probe shows max |delta| >= 0.08 under the pinned hash and model, which indicates the frozen scorer is no longer frozen and voids the comparison.
